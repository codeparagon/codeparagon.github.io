A private letter

Nayab

نایاب

rare. precious. the only one.

Tap the seal

A letter written only for you

Nayab

The wax is waiting for you.

Written with all that I am

Meri Nayab,

Yours, always — Ahmar

Why you

You are the poem I could never finish

01

Your name

Nayab means rare. The universe named you before I ever found you — and it was right.

02

Your light

In a noisy world you are stillness. In an ordinary day you are the reason it becomes holy.

03

Your forever

I do not want a moment with you. I want the mornings, the prayers, the winters, the children of our laughter.

تم میری دعا ہو، میری پناہ ہو، میری نایاب ہو

Nayab

کیا تم مجھ سے شادی کرو گی؟

Will you marry me?

Not for a season. For every life I am given.

She said yes

Nayab

اب ہماری کہانی شروع ہوتی ہے

You just made me the luckiest man alive.

From this night forward, every sunrise has your name in it. I will spend the rest of my days proving that choosing me was the easiest yes of your life.

Forever yours — Ahmar
